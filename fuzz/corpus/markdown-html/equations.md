# Equations

Inline: the ratio $\frac{a}{b}$, the power $x^2$, the index $x_i$, both $x_i^2$ and the root $\sqrt{x}$.

Greek and symbols inline: $\alpha + \beta \leq \gamma \neq \infty$ and $\pm \times \cdot \approx$.

Display fraction:

$$\frac{a+b}{c-d}$$

Quadratic formula:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Nth root:

$$\sqrt[3]{x+1}$$

Sum with limits:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i^2$$

Integral with limits:

$$\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx$$

Double integral and product:

$$\iint_D f \, dA \qquad \prod_{k=1}^{m} a_k$$

Matrix in parentheses:

$$\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$$

Matrix in brackets:

$$\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$

Determinant bars:

$$\begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix}$$

Aligned equations:

$$\begin{aligned} f(x) &= (x+1)^2 \\ &= x^2 + 2x + 1 \end{aligned}$$

Functions:

$$\sin x + \cos^2 \theta = \ln y$$

Limit:

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$$

Accents:

$$\hat{x} + \vec{v} + \bar{y} + \tilde{z} + \dot{w}$$

Overline and underline:

$$\overline{AB} + \underline{CD}$$

Binomial:

$$\binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}$$

Cases:

$$f(x) = \begin{cases} x & x \geq 0 \\ -x & x < 0 \end{cases}$$

Braces and text:

$$\underbrace{a+b}_{\text{sum}} \quad \overbrace{c+d}^{n}$$

Set notation:

$$A \cup B \subseteq C, \quad x \in \mathbb{R}, \quad \forall x \exists y$$

Left and right delimiters:

$$\left( \frac{a}{b} \right) \left\{ x \right\} \left\langle y \right\rangle$$

Arrows:

$$a \rightarrow b \Rightarrow c \leftrightarrow d$$

Text and special characters:

$$x \text{ and } y \quad \mathrm{d}x \quad \partial f / \partial x$$

Final inline: the value $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ is famous.
